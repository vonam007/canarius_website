How Canarius Agents Execute Work

A 5-layer deterministic pipeline engineered for enterprise safety, observability, and sub-second reasoning latency.

STAGE 01

Ingestion & Intent

Natural language incoming request is sanitized, classified, and mapped to business intent schemas.

STAGE 02

Vector Retrieval

Hybrid dense/sparse search pulls relevant enterprise policies and real-time database state.

STAGE 03

Agent Reasoning

Multi-step planner plans necessary actions, evaluating edge cases and security guardrails.

STAGE 04

Authenticated Tool Call

The agent triggers external APIs (Stripe, CRM, ERP) with structured JSON schemas and auth tokens.

STAGE 05

Audit & Response

Action outcome is validated against policy bounds, logged in telemetry, and summarized to user.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

How does a newly registered business get approved for the free Startup Grant? ▼
To guarantee approval, submit your valid Tax ID (Mã số thuế) proving the business was registered within the last 24 months, apply using your company domain email (@yourcompany.com), provide a link to your website/landing page, and briefly describe your intended use case. Eligible startups are approved within 24 hours.
How does Canarius guarantee AI agents won't hallucinate critical actions? ▼
We combine structured output decoding with deterministic policy guardrails. Agents cannot trigger an external API tool unless all required parameters pass strict Pydantic/Zod schema validation and satisfy pre-configured business boundary constraints (such as maximum refund dollar limits).
Can Canarius be deployed within our private VPC or on-premise infrastructure? ▼
Yes. For Enterprise tier clients, we offer Docker and Kubernetes Helm charts that deploy directly into your AWS, GCP, or Azure Virtual Private Cloud (VPC), ensuring your sensitive business data never leaves your perimeter.
How do human staff supervise or intervene in agent workflows? ▼
Canarius provides built-in Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) triggers. You can define confidence thresholds or specific sensitive operations (e.g. wire transfers or contracts over $10k) that pause execution and prompt a human manager for one-click Slack/Teams approval.
How long does an initial pilot implementation take? ▼
Most teams deploy their first operational agent within 5 to 10 business days using our turnkey connectors for Zendesk, Notion, PostgreSQL, and custom REST APIs.
